# Code of conduct

InputFlip is a small project, and we want everyone who reports a monitor, asks a question or sends a pull request to
feel welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here aren't developers; they just want their monitor to switch.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Credit others' work.

## What isn't OK

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or abilities.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or repeatedly derailing discussions.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue (in its "..." menu), or open a short issue asking the
maintainer ([undah](https://github.com/undah)) to get in touch, without details, and you'll be offered a private way to
talk. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people
who don't follow this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
